The Phased Implementation Roadmap

A pragmatic, phased approach focused on delivering measurable IT efficiency first, then scaling business value across the organization.

Phase 1: Build the Factory Foundation

(Months 1-3)

Objective

Establish the governance and processes for the Data Factory and prove its effectiveness with a single, high-value pilot integration pipeline.

Key Deliverables
  • AIGC Established: AI Governance Committee is chartered and key members are assigned.
  • Data Factory Playbook: The standard operating procedure for data integration is defined and approved.
  • Pilot Integration Complete: Onboard one new data source using the playbook, measuring baseline metrics.

Phase 2: Scale the Factory & Show Business Value

(Months 3-6)

Objective

Move from a successful pilot to proven value by scaling the Data Factory, quantifying its ROI, and using the newly available data to launch the first high-impact business intelligence project.

Key Deliverables
  • Prove Scalability: Onboard 3-5 new, high-priority data sources using the factory model to clear the existing backlog.
  • Quantify ROI: Launch a live ROI dashboard tracking the reduction in time-to-insight and engineering effort.
  • Deliver First Business Win: Empower the highest-impact department with a Conversational BI pilot, allowing them to ask questions of their new data in plain English.

Phase 3: Democratize Data & Advanced Insights

(Months 6-12)

Objective

Scale proven AI capabilities across the organization to democratize data access and develop more advanced, predictive insights.

Key Deliverables
  • Organization Rollout of Conversational BI: Expand access to Tableau's natural language features to majority of the business lines.
  • Launch Proactive Insights: Deploy Tableau Pulse to deliver automated, proactive alerts on KPI changes to key stakeholders.
  • Launch First Custom AI-Powered Project: Partner with a key business line to develop a targeted AI solution for a high-value problem.

Phased Business Impact & ROI

This initiative is measured by concrete IT efficiency gains that directly translate into accelerated business value.
