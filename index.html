The Phased Implementation Roadmap

A detailed, multi-year view of how the Generative AI initiative will scale across the enterprise, building compounding value over time.

Phase 1: Foundational Readiness & Governance

(Months 1-6)

Objective

To build the non-negotiable technical and governance bedrock for all future AI success, focusing on IT and risk management.

Target Departments

Information Technology, Compliance, Legal, Risk.

Key Deliverables
  • AI Governance Committee established and chartered.
  • Pilot AI-assisted data integration pipeline executed.
  • "Factory Model" Playbook v1.0 for scalable data integration.

Phase 2: Initial Value Realization

(Months 6-12)

Objective

To deploy the first wave of high-impact applications, building trust and proving the reusability of the architecture.

Target Departments

Compliance, Investment Research, Wealth Management.

Key Deliverables
  • Compliance Research Assistant (RAG Application) deployed.
  • Automated Earnings Call Summaries for Investment Research.
  • Conversational Analytics Pilot for select Wealth Advisors.

Phase 3: Enterprise Scaling & Optimization

(Months 12-24)

Objective

To scale proven AI patterns to other business units and deliver proactive insights to senior leadership.

Target Departments

Operations, Executive Leadership, Commercial Lending.

Key Deliverables
  • Automated AML Alert Triage for Operations.
  • Conversational BI for Executive Leadership via Tableau Pulse.
  • Enhanced Credit Risk Model Pilot for Commercial Lending.

Phase 4: Continuous Innovation & Maturation

(Months 24+)

Objective

To embed GenAI as a core capability and explore advanced applications that create a sustainable competitive advantage.

Target Departments

All, including M&A and Corporate Strategy.

Key Deliverables
  • Enterprise-wide "AI Co-pilot" availability.
  • Automated Due Diligence for M&A team.
  • Exploration of custom Cortex Agents for complex tasks.
